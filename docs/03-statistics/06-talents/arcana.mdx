### Wild Overload

<TalentRank rank={1}>

When you cast an arcane spell of rank 1 or higher, you can choose to overload it with wild arcane energy before making the spell attack roll. If you do, roll 1d6 before your casting roll and apply the result:

- **1.** Roll on the Wild Surge table (d100) and apply the result directly after your spell’s effect.
- **2.** The spell's target or target location is randomly determined (roll randomly among valid targets within range, or shift the target location 1d4 close distances in a random direction). If you fail your spell roll, you lose half the spell’s Focus cost instead.
- **3.** You suffer +1 bane on your spell roll.
- **4.** You gain +1 boon on your spell roll.
- **5.** Heighten the spell by one rank. If the spell requires a static target Difficulty, it is also increased by one rank.
