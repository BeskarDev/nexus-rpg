# Abilities

Print the full rules text of a character's talents, folk abilities and one-off abilities. A talent card carries the whole rank ladder; switch on **Only the ranks a character owns** to cut each one to what its character has bought.

Combat arts are not here on purpose — they have [their own deck](./combat-arts).
