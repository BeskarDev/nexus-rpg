# 🎴 Creature Cards

Print any creature in the bestiary as a card. The whole catalogue is in the tool, so preparing an encounter no longer means copying a stat block out of the docs and pasting it back in. Use the **tier** and **category** filters to find what you need, then pick creatures one at a time.

Two other sources sit beside the catalogue: **load a character's companions**, and **paste creature markdown** for something written in your own notes.

Each card carries the creature's defenses, hit points with life-pool pips, armor value and its four attribute dice, then its traits, attacks, abilities and quick actions. A creature too long for one card continues onto a second, which repeats the name and its part mark so a card found loose on the table still says what it belongs to.
