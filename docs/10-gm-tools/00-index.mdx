# ⚙️ GM Tools

![banner-img](/img/banner/gm-tools-banner.png)

What a GM reaches for at the table: oracles to roll on when the party goes somewhere you did not prepare, printable references to keep beside the screen, and builders for the things you make between sessions.

## Random Tables

Ask a table a question and it answers. Every one of these rolls in the browser, and every one keeps its full data behind a fold so you can roll it by hand instead.

<ToolIndex>
	<ToolEntry href="./random-tables/random-treasure" name="Treasures" sigil="chalice">A hoard by tier: what it is, what it is made of, what it is worth.</ToolEntry>
	<ToolEntry href="./random-tables/random-creature" name="Creatures" sigil="paw">A beast that fits the place, with how it hunts and what it leaves behind.</ToolEntry>
	<ToolEntry href="./random-tables/random-spell" name="Spells" sigil="sparkle">A spell name and effect, by discipline or tradition.</ToolEntry>
	<ToolEntry href="./random-tables/random-name" name="Names" sigil="cartouche">Personal and family names for all twelve cultures.</ToolEntry>
	<ToolEntry href="./random-tables/random-challenge" name="Challenges" sigil="standard">A puzzle, a trap, or a combat scene with a twist in it.</ToolEntry>
	<ToolEntry href="./random-tables/random-quests" name="Quest Hooks" sigil="scroll">Who asks, what they want, and what complicates it.</ToolEntry>
	<ToolEntry href="./random-tables/social-intrigue" name="NPCs" sigil="two-faces">Someone to talk to: what they want, how they carry themselves, what they will not do.</ToolEntry>
	<ToolEntry href="./random-tables/random-settlement" name="Settlements" sigil="temple">A town and its ground, its rulers, its trouble.</ToolEntry>
	<ToolEntry href="./random-tables/random-terrain" name="Terrain and Locations" sigil="mountains">Where the party is standing, and what is strange about it.</ToolEntry>
</ToolIndex>
