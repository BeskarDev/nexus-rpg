### Clay Servant

<CreatureLore>

A knee-high figure of fired clay with a hoe moulded into its fists, buried by the hundred so the dead would not have to work their own fields. ### Ghoul

<CreatureLore>

A thin, quick eater of the dead, at home in the necropolis and in the graves the robbers opened first. They talk, in a fashion, and it has never once been a conversation. What they want is always the same thing, and a living person is the part of it that is still moving. The grave-wardens of the old cities know which colonies are theirs and leave a body out at the end of the season.
