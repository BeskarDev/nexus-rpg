### Orc Band-Leader

<CreatureLore>

<LoreSection label="Tactics">They stay behind the line and run it. `Fight Where I Point` sets up whichever raider is best placed. `Take It On The Shield` puts the leader in the way of a blow meant for one of their raiders. Wounding them changes the fight rather than ending it. `Blood Up` takes a careful commander and turns them into something that trades hits, so the second half is more dangerous and much shorter. **They can be bought at any point before that.** A party that offers the grain is taking the deal the leader would have proposed themselves.</LoreSection>
