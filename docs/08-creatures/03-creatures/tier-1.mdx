### Grave Husk

<CreatureLore>

One of the unburied dead, walking in the body nobody said the words over. They do not remember being a person, and nothing in them wants anything. They walk a short distance from where they were left and no further. They keep doing it until somebody puts them in the ground.

<LoreSection label="Ecology"><LoreTag>Desert</LoreTag><LoreTag>Grassland</LoreTag><LoreTag>Battlefield</LoreTag><LoreTag>Necropolis</LoreTag><LoreTag>Barrow</LoreTag> They do not eat, sleep, or rest, and they do not decay the way a body should. They rise where rites were skipped: plague pits, battlefields nobody came back for, robbed graves, any burial that was interrupted. They cannot be reasoned with or frightened. That, not their strength, is the practical problem. One husk on a road costs a village a week and a priest's fee. A field of them after a battle is why the temples send people.</LoreSection>

<LoreSection label="Physiology"><LoreTag>Whatever the person was in life, and most of them stood 1.6 to 1.8 m</LoreTag><LoreTag>40 to 60 kg, and falling, since they dry out rather than rot</LoreTag><LoreTag>They do not age, and a husk still walking a road may be 200 years dead</LoreTag></LoreSection>

<LoreSection label="Tactics">They walk at whoever is closest and take hold. **Grasping Hands** grapples on a strong hit and they do not let go. Once they have hold, switch to **Bearing Down** and use it every turn after, which puts the held character prone. They never speed up, never flank and never choose a better target. They cannot follow past the ground they were left on, and **Grave-Bound** says how far that reaches. They do not rout. Killing one settles nothing, since they stand up again at the next sunset unless the body is buried or burned.</LoreSection>

### Goblin Skulker

<CreatureLore>

A goblin who has learned that nobody watches the small one until it is too late, and who has never once picked a fight they were expected to win. They are not really trying to kill anybody. They are trying to leave with something, and the knife is only there in case that goes wrong.

<LoreSection label="Ecology"><LoreTag>Desert</LoreTag><LoreTag>Grassland</LoreTag><LoreTag>Hills</LoreTag><LoreTag>Road</LoreTag><LoreTag>Ruins</LoreTag><LoreTag>Caves</LoreTag><LoreTag>Den</LoreTag> They live where a road runs past cover, which is most roads. A gang holds a stretch of it for a season, sleeps in whatever hole is nearest, and moves on when the carters start travelling armed. They eat what they take, and what they take is mostly food. Nothing about this is a war. The stretch of road matters more than the goblins on it, and a village that loses two carts in a month knows exactly which bend the trouble is at and cannot spare the men to go and look. Carters, drovers, tax collectors and pilgrims are the people who meet them, and most of the ones who do arrive late rather than not at all.</LoreSection>

<LoreSection label="Physiology"><LoreTag>0.92 to 0.98 m tall</LoreTag><LoreTag>17 to 23 kg</LoreTag><LoreTag>Adult at 12, old at 35, and 50 years is a long life</LoreTag></LoreSection>

<LoreSection label="Tactics">They do not open the fight. They wait for somebody else to, then come in from the side. **Snatch** is what they are here for, so spend their turns robbing whoever is already busy and save the knife for when somebody corners them. They take from the character with the most hanging off their belt, not the most dangerous one, and they leave with it. **Pack Tactics** wants another goblin already on the same target, so put two of them on one character rather than one each on two. Between turns they spend **Quick Escape** on the first attack that misses them. They will not stand in a line and they will not hold ground. One of them alone breaks and runs, and a gang that has lost half its number goes with it, carrying whatever it has.</LoreSection>

### Goblin Archer

<CreatureLore>

The one in the rocks above the road, who has been watching the track since the last bend. They shoot first, and they shoot from somewhere nobody has thought to look.

The bow is the one thing in a gang that gets looked after, because it is the one thing they cannot make again in an afternoon, and the goblin holding it is usually the one who has been on this road longest. <LoreSection label="Physiology"><LoreTag>0.92 to 0.98 m tall</LoreTag><LoreTag>17 to 23 kg</LoreTag><LoreTag>Adult at 12, old at 35, and 50 years is a long life</LoreTag></LoreSection>

<LoreSection label="Tactics">They open the fight, from cover, at whatever range the ground gives them. They shoot whoever is least protected rather than whoever is most dangerous, and they keep shooting the same one. They give up height only when they have to. ### Young Crocodile

<CreatureLore>

A river crocodile not yet at full size, small enough to lie up under a landing stage. River villages keep their children off the bank at dusk, and the fence along the bank is there for the same reason.

<LoreSection label="Ecology"><LoreTag>River</LoreTag><LoreTag>Marsh</LoreTag><LoreTag>Settlement</LoreTag> They lie up in shallow water against a bank, a jetty, or a stand of reed, and take whatever comes down to drink. A young one hunts dogs, goats, waterfowl, and people who are alone. They avoid anything in a group. Full-grown crocodiles drive the young off the best stretches, so the ones a village meets are usually this size and usually hungry. One that has taken livestock keeps using the same crossing until something changes. The village always knows where they are.</LoreSection>

<LoreSection label="Physiology"><LoreTag>2.2 m long</LoreTag><LoreTag>70 kg</LoreTag><LoreTag>60 years or more, and one this size is 4 to 6 years old</LoreTag> The female buries forty eggs in the warm sand of a bank and guards the nest until they hatch. Almost none of them reach this size, and the ones that do have already been driven off the best water by something larger.</LoreSection>

<LoreSection label="Tactics">They lie submerged and wait for somebody to come to the bank rather than swimming into view. Open with the **Bite** against a target who does not know they are there, since **Ambush Predator** raises that first strike one success level. The bite grapples, and the grapple is the point, because a grappled person is going into the river. They cannot hold and bite at once, so a crocodile with someone in their jaws spends the turn dragging and attacks nobody else. If the fight turns they release, submerge, and do not come back while anyone is standing on their bank.</LoreSection>
