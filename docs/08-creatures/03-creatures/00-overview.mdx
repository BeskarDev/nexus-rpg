## Creature Tiers

Creatures are organized by tier (0-10), representing their power level and appropriate challenge for adventurers. Each tier corresponds to a character level, making encounter balance straightforward. A single creature of a given tier should provide a decent challenge for one adventurer of the same level.

### Basic vs. Elite vs. Lord

- **Basic**: Standard creatures with one life pool, suitable for individual encounters or minions
- **Elite**: Tougher creatures with two life pools (2×HP), providing mid-boss challenges
- **Lord**: Major threats with three life pools (3×HP), designed as climactic encounters

### Encounter Building

[Building Encounters](../../10-gm-tools/02-builder-tools/12-building-encounters.md) measures a fight in **Threat Points**, counting for:

- **Party size**: More adventurers need more creatures
- **Tier difference**: A creature above the party's level is worth several at it
- **Creature category**: Elite and Lord creatures are worth multiple basic creatures
- **Terrain and tactics**: Environmental factors can shift difficulty a whole step
