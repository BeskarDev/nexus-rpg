## Playstyle

The Summoner controls tempo with glyphs and durable constructs while adding bodies to the field. Arcane Bolt costs no Focus and dazes on a strong hit, so you always have a turn. Arcane Glyph is a trap you lay before the fight, and Arcane Barrier is a door of force you drop into a corridor mid-fight to decide who reaches whom. Master of Fundamentals re-rolls one of your free spells each scene. Conjure Familiar is a ritual of hours costing 100 coins of incense, which your starting purse covers, so you can begin play with your familiar already beside you. Later, Summon Aberration adds real battlefield presence.

## Advancement

Raise Arcana 2-3 for more spells and Focus. Consider Inexhaustible Mind or Spellweaver. Add Education for arcane theory and Streetwise for the places a familiar can scout but you cannot walk. After your familiar, look to Summon Aberration and utility control like Arcane Eye.
