## Playstyle

The Apothecary rewards patience, setup, and smart resource use. Open from stealth with the silent blowpipe. Coat darts with Weapon Poison and use the blowpipe property to multiply the poison’s damage on strong/critical hits. Peak Performance lets you boost your darts (ammo) for +2 damage the whole day, turning small hits into meaningful chip. Poison Maker lets you brew your own weapon poisons in the field, so your damage never depends on what you could afford to buy: spend an exploration turn, one use of materials, and roll Spirit or Mind + Nature. Use Aimed Shot to land reliable first strikes and Precise Shot to punch through armor when needed.

## Advancement

Raise Archery 2-3 for more Combat Arts and consistency (Sharpshooter or Rapid Shot next). Push Nature 2-3 for higher-Quality poisons and a second brew per rest (Field Medic next if the party wants a medic), and Crafting 2-3 for stronger prep (Efficient Worker, Artisan, or Peak Performance 2). Add Lore for formula lore and Education for scholarly support. Consider Stealth later to exploit blowpipe ambushes with Assassination synergy.
