## Playstyle

The Warlord is perfect for players who want to lead the party tactically, enhancing allies rather than focusing on personal combat. The glaive has reach, so you strike over the front rank without standing in it, which is what keeps your Quick Action free for Commander every single round. Commander is the whole build: an ally gets +1 Movement or +1 boon on their next roll, on their turn, from you. Leading Presence costs nothing and hands the whole party boons on Initiative and against fear. Disciplined Fighter is your one free re-roll on a melee attack, so it never competes with commanding. Use Exhilarating Strike to heal allies on a hit and Defensive Strike when something reaches you anyway.

## Advancement

Increase Fighting and Influence to rank 2-3 for more Combat Arts and leadership abilities, raise Education for tactical options, add Fortitude for survivability, and consider Polearm Mastery to make the reach work harder.
