## Playstyle

The Monk is perfect for players who want to fight without weapons or armor, using martial arts and grappling. Pugilist makes your fists count as brawling weapons, and it does the same for simple weapons, so your quarterstaff is part of the style rather than a compromise: +1 Parry either way, and Grappler and both your Combat Arts work with staff or empty hands. Body of Bronze gives you 1 + Fortitude as armor while you wear none, and a helmet is still allowed, so you stand at AV 3 with nothing on your body. Grappler turns any hit into a grapple, which is what Choking Grip hangs off, and Knockout can put a heavily armored enemy on the floor entirely.

## Advancement

Increase Fighting and Athletics to rank 2-3 for more Combat Arts and grappling options, raise Fortitude for more toughness, add Insight for reading opponents, and take Martial Artist next: it makes your unarmed attacks and your staff count as agile, which moves your whole style onto Agility if you would rather be quick than strong.
