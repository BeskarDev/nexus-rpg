## Playstyle

The Warlock is perfect for players who enjoy morally complex characters with manipulative magic and dark themes. Your power is borrowed, not earned, and it strains against you. Wild Overload lets you push a rank 1 spell past its safe limits when a fight needs to end now, at the risk of a wild surge. Mage Hunter turns your own damage into a weapon against enemy spellcasters, because every point you deal makes their concentration harder to hold. Out of combat, Read the Room hands you what a person wants or fears, which is where mind control actually starts.

## Advancement

Increase Arcana to rank 2-3 for more powerful spells and additional spells known, raise Lore for deeper occult knowledge, add Fortitude to survive your own backlash, and consider Spellweaver for Metamagic Arts.
