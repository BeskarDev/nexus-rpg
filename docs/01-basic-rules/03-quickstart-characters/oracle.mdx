## Playstyle

The Oracle is perfect for players who enjoy support roles, being the party's guide, and themes of fate and prophecy. Between scenes you are the party's compass: Thread of Fate names which of two courses holds the better fortune, and Take Their Measure has the GM tell you what a creature will do next, what it fears, or where it is weakest. In a fight you are not helpless. Stern Gaze costs no Focus and hits their Resist, and it deals more and dazes them once your Light magic has already blinded or unmasked them. Moment of Clarity is a Quick Action that lets an ally throw off a charm or a fright, and Curse of Twilight leaves an enemy half blind and slow to act. Divine Favor is the blessing itself: pray at rest, then spend Favor with no action at all to put a boon on an ally's roll at the moment it matters.

## Advancement

Increase Mysticism and Lore to rank 2-3 for more powerful spells and deeper knowledge, raise Insight for better intuition, learn more divination and revelation spells, add Education for broader knowledge, and take Divine Rites so you can pray your Focus back during a break. Detect Lies and Blessing of Light are the revelations you skipped at creation and are worth picking up early.
