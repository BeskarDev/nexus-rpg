## Playstyle

The Barbarian is perfect for players who want to deal massive damage and play aggressively, accepting risk for reward. Battle Rage provides combat bonuses while Heavy Weapon Mastery makes Brutal Strike attacks deadly without suffering banes. Bulky gives you extra HP to survive when rage runs out. You wear no armor at all, because rage requires it: a shield or helmet is still allowed, but your greataxe needs both hands. A helmet is the one piece of protection you can still wear, and it is where your coins went: AV 1 from turn one, with your HP doing the rest of the work. Use Cleave to hit multiple foes and Brutal Strike for single-target devastation.

## Advancement

Raise Fortitude to rank 2 first and take Body of Bronze: unarmored, it gives you 1 + Fortitude as AV, which is the armor your rage forbids you to buy. Then increase Battle Rage for greater power, raise Fighting to rank 2-3 for more Combat Arts, add Survival for wilderness expertise, and consider Hard to Kill for surviving what gets through.
