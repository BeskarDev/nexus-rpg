## Playstyle

The Tamer locks lanes and creates openings for their animal. Your whip has reach, so you fight from close range and Riposte punishes anyone who swings at you and misses. Grappler turns any whip hit into a grapple, which is what Snare hangs off: restrained, and prone on a strong hit, with your companion already on top of them. Disarm strips the threat out of their hands entirely. Your companion is your second body. Set up flanks and force bad choices.

## Advancement

Raise Nature 2-3 to deepen companion options. Fighting 2-3 for more Combat Arts (Supreme Disarm, Flurry). Add Survival for fieldcraft and Fast Stride from Athletics if you would rather kite than hold a lane. Consider Field Medic from Nature for downtime care.
