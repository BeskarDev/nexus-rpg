## Playstyle

The Slinger is perfect for players who want a mobile, hit-and-run playstyle with simple but effective ranged combat. Harass from short range, then reposition with Fast Stride. Your arts exist to keep the distance you just made: Pinning Shot drops their Movement to zero, and Disabling Shot works because a sling is crush, leaving them dazed instead of chasing you. The bola is the answer to whoever closes anyway: it entangles anything up to large size, and your Strength meets its requirement exactly. Ammo Specialist keeps your stones from running out, and Leading the Way means the whole party moves unseen when you scout ahead, not just you. Watch your load. At 11 you are exactly at capacity, and going over would cost you the Dash Action that Fast Stride is for.

## Advancement

Increase Archery to rank 2-3 for more Combat Arts and damage, raise Athletics for mobility, add Survival for scouting, and consider Rapid Shot, which works because a sling is not heavy. Snare is worth learning once you carry the bola: an entangling throw counts as a grapple, which is what that art hangs off. Keep coins spare, since every bundle you throw depletes.
