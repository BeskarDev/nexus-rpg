## Playstyle

The Magus is perfect for players who want to combine melee combat with spellcasting in a hybrid warrior-mage. Spellblade is the archetype in one line: spend your Quick Action and 2 extra Focus, and a rank 0 spell rides your longsword instead of being cast at range. Both of your disciplines carry two rank 0 spells that qualify, and Focus 8 pays for it three times a scene. Your two arts split the enemy roster and Blade Mastery is the switch between them: against armor, Quick Lunge strikes at their Dodge and denies them a reaction; against light or no armor, flip the longsword to slash and Deep Cut leaves them bleeding. Eloquent Talker means the scholar half of you talks with Mind and Education instead of the Spirit you do not have.

## Advancement

Increase Arcana and Fighting equally to rank 2-3 for more spells and Combat Arts, and take Battle Mage next: at AV 2 with Dodge 7, a Quick Action barrier for 2 Focus is the defence you gave up to get Spellblade at creation. Add Lore for magical knowledge, and Mana Shield once you cast rank 1 spells often.
