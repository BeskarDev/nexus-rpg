## Playstyle

The Fighter is perfect for players who want straightforward, effective combat with tactical depth. A greatsword deals 4 weapon damage and reaches two extra enemies with Cleave, so a knot of foes is where you want to stand. You have no shield, so you hold the line by being the one worth hitting: Divert Attention turns an attack into a Distract, and Protect Ally still lets you step in front of a blow and take it on Parry 9. Bulky asks for no action at all and leaves you with 24 HP, the most in the set, and Second Wind gives 4 of them back once a scene. Art of Fighting is why you have five Combat Arts instead of three, which is the real difference between a Fighter and everyone else who swings a sword. The javelin plus Aimed Shot answers the enemy you cannot reach.

## Advancement

Increase Fighting and Fortitude to rank 2-3 for more Combat Arts and resilience, add Athletics for mobility under a heavy weapon, and consider Heavy Weapon Mastery or Blade Mastery to widen what the greatsword can answer.
