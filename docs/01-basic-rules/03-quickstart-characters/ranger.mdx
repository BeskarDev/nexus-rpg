## Playstyle

The Ranger is perfect for players who enjoy ranged combat, wilderness survival, and having an animal companion. Strike from distance with precision while your animal companion provides tactical support. Your longbow hits for 8, 12 or 16 and Sharpshooter carries it out past its normal range, so pick your ground and make them cross it. Against anything bigger than you, spend a Quick Action on Monster Hunter first: +2 damage for a short duration, which is the difference between wounding a beast and dropping it. Aimed Shot rewards holding still, Precise Shot cuts through armor, and Evasive Strike is there for the moment something reaches you: hit it, then break back to range.

## Advancement

Increase Archery to rank 2-3 for more Combat Arts and damage, raise Survival and Nature for wilderness mastery, add Perception for better awareness, and consider Disciplined Archer or Reflexive Shooter, which keeps your bow working when enemies close. Relentless Tracker is worth picking up once Survival reaches rank 2 if tracking is your table's currency. Rapid Shot needs a non-heavy bow, so it does nothing for a longbow.
