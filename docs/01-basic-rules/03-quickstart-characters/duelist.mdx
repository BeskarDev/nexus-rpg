## Playstyle

The Duelist is perfect for players who prefer agility and precision over brute force. Your shortsword is agile, so Agility carries both the attack and the damage, and every point of it works twice. Quick Lunge goes around a shield entirely by striking at Dodge, and it denies the reaction an armored opponent was counting on. Precise Strike ignores half their armor when the moment matters. Devious Tactics turns Distract into a Quick Action, and Light Armor Mastery gives you a free Evade each scene, so the one attack that gets through you meets Dodge 9 instead of Parry.

## Advancement

Increase Fighting and Athletics to rank 2-3, add Stealth or Influence depending on intrigue vs. street play, and consider Riposte to punish the attacks you make miss. A Twinblade is the agile upgrade worth saving for, but only once Dual Wielder is on your sheet.
