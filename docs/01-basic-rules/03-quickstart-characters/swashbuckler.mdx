## Playstyle

The Swashbuckler thrives on movement and momentum. Dual Wielder means both blades swing in one Action with no banes, and every hit is 6 to 10 damage plus 2 more from the poison you coated them with. Your whole game is denying answers: the Swashbuckler talent stops an enemy you hit from using Quick Actions against you for the turn, and Quick Lunge denies the reaction to the attack itself while striking at their Dodge instead of their Parry. Disarm takes the weapon out of their hand entirely. Light Armor Mastery gives you a free Evade each scene, so the one attack that finds you meets Dodge 9.

## Advancement

Raise Fighting to rank 2-3 for more Combat Arts (Supreme Feint, Quick Lunge, or Riposte). Boost Athletics for mobility or Streetwise for Thug Tactics if you prefer gang-up brawling. Stealth is worth adding if you want the ambush half of the street as well as the duel. Consider Defensive Dueling if you pivot to single-blade finesse. Add Fortitude for toughness and Influence for swagger-driven social play.
