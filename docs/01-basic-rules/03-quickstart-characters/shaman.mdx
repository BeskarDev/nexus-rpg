## Playstyle

The Shaman is perfect for players who enjoy spiritual themes, supporting through blessings, and connecting with ancestral powers. Bridge the physical and spirit worlds. Commune with Ancestors is what the archetype is named for: pour a libation, name your honored dead, and describe the problem that weighs on you, and the spirits answer. In a fight your magic costs nothing to start: Minor Hex hangs a small doom on an enemy so their next roll suffers a bane, and Spared from Death lets you reach across a room to a dying ally and add to the roll that decides whether they live. Healing Touch is the one spell you spend Focus on, and it mends what a salve cannot. Divine Sense finds the spirits and undead nobody else can see, and Herbalist keeps your people supplied with remedies between the fights.

## Advancement

Increase Mysticism to rank 2-3 for more powerful spells, raise Lore for deeper spiritual knowledge, add Insight for reading people and spirits, and learn Curse of Death and Glimpse of Mortality as your curses deepen. Field Medic is worth taking at Nature rank 2 if your party has no other healer.
