## Playstyle

Close distance and take away your opponent’s best options. A cestus in each fist is the whole point: Dual Wielder means you swing both without the banes that punish anyone else for trying. Disarm strips a weapon out of their hands, and Head Smack leaves them confused when the skull rings. Thug Tactics rewards ganging up, so fight where your allies are. You won’t out-damage a greataxe and you aren’t meant to: you take away what the enemy was going to do, and let the party finish it.

## Advancement

Raise Fighting 2-3 for more control arts (Supreme Disarm) and improved damage lines. Add Fortitude for toughness and recovery, and consider Streetwise 2 for better gang-up play. A spiked chain extends your control footprint once your technique improves, though it costs you the second fist. Pugilist is also open later if you ever want to fight with nothing in your hands at all.
