## Playstyle

The Gladiator is perfect for players who want to control a fight rather than end it quickly. The spiked chain has reach, so you strike from close range and they have to come to you. Grappler turns any hit into a grapple, which is what Snare hangs off: restrained, and prone on a strong hit. Felling Strike puts them in the sand without a grapple at all. You will not out-damage a greataxe and you are not trying to: the enemy you have tangled is the one your party kills. Leading Presence works the crowd and the line at the same time.

## Advancement

Increase Fighting to rank 2-3 for more Combat Arts, raise Athletics to hold your grapples against stronger foes, and add Fortitude for resilience. Choking Grip is the next art worth having, and Banded Leather is the first purchase your spare coins should go to.
