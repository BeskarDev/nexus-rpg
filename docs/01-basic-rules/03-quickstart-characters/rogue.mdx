## Playstyle

The Rogue is perfect for players who enjoy stealth, infiltration, skill-based challenges, and being the party's scout. Everything you do is built around one strike. Set up unseen, hold still so Feint gives you +1 boon, and open with Assassination for +2 damage on an enemy who has no idea you are there, with poison on the blade for another +2 that ignores armor entirely. Disciplined Fighter re-rolls that attack if it misses, because it only comes once a scene. Then Evasive Strike lets you hit and break away without an opportunity attack, back to somewhere they have to find you again. Out of a fight, Thieves' Tools and Jack of All Trades mean there are very few doors, locks or conversations you have no answer for.

## Advancement

Increase Stealth and Fighting to rank 2-3 for more options and Combat Arts, add Insight for reading people, and take Infiltrator to make locks and traps your specialty or Shadow Slip to slide out of the one attack that finds you. Blade Mastery is worth revisiting once you learn Deep Cut, which it turns on.
