## Playstyle

The Engineer is perfect for players who enjoy battlefield problem‑solving and logistics. Bomb Maker turns an exploration turn and a use of materials into ordnance: thunderstones to deafen a room, smoke to break line of sight, fire bombs for a packed corridor. Every bomb is a thrown weapon, so your Archery delivers it, Aimed Shot makes the important throw land, and Volley scatters one across three enemies. Expert Slinger is what keeps you supplied, since every bundle you throw forces a Supply check after the fight. Tactician means the fight starts on your terms: scout it first and the party opens with boons.

## Advancement

Increase Crafting to rank 2-3 for higher-Quality bombs and a second build per rest, add Perception for scouting, and raise Archery for more Combat Arts and range. Efficient Worker keeps your materials from running dry, and Quick Construction is worth taking once your Strength can carry it.
