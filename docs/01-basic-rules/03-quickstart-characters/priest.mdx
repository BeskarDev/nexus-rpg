## Playstyle

The Priest is perfect for players who want to support allies with healing and protection while remaining combat-effective. You wear no armor on purpose: Armor of the Faithful gives you 1 + Mysticism as your armor, and a shield still counts as wearing none, so you stand at AV 3 in a robe. As your Mysticism grows, so does your skin. You are the party's healer first and everything else second, and the only Devotion of Life in the set: nobody mends a wound like you do. Your mace and shield exist so that standing near the fighting is survivable, since your healing reaches only as far as your hand does, and the mace is crush, so it ignores half of whatever armor your enemies wear. Rallying Cry pulls an ally out of a fright or a daze for a Quick Action, and Exhilarating Strike is the one attack worth making: a hit heals you and every ally close to you. Defensive Strike keeps you standing, and Exhilarating Strike heals you and every ally near you on a hit, which is the closest thing in the game to fighting and healing in the same breath.

## Advancement

Increase Mysticism to rank 2-3 for more powerful spells, raise Influence for stronger authority and rallying, take Fighting to rank 1 if you want a second Combat Art and a steadier Parry, and learn Rapid Vitality or Sustaining Grace when you tire of spending your whole Action to heal: both are Quick Actions, where Healing Touch is slow but heals far more. Divine Rites is worth taking once your spells cost more Focus than a fight can spare.
