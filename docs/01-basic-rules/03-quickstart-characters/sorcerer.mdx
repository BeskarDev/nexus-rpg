## Playstyle

The Sorcerer is perfect for players who want to deal massive magical damage and control the battlefield through spells. Unleash raw arcane power. Flickering Flame and Frost Snap cost no Focus at all, so your ordinary turn is 6 to 10 damage for free, and Chromatic Orb is what you spend Focus on: it picks its damage type as you cast, so nothing resists you twice. Elemental Ward is your only defence in the moment, a Quick Action reaction that adds +2 to the Defense being attacked, and Inexhaustible Mind means a short break puts half your Focus back and the artillery keeps firing. You have 16 HP and AV 2. Stand behind someone.

## Advancement

Increase Arcana to rank 2-3 for more powerful spells and additional spells known, add Insight for better awareness, and consider Spellweaver for Metamagic or Mana Shield once your spells are rank 2 and the barrier it grants is worth having. Adopting a second discipline widens what you can answer, at the cost of depth in Evocation.
