# Gods, Mystics and Arcanists in Art

Read for any image with a god, a temple, a divine symbol, a mystic or an arcanist in it. The
period rules are in [art-setting.md](art-setting.md), the folk who staff these scenes in
[art-folk.md](art-folk.md). The palette rule that decides what colour magic is — **cyan is
arcane, warm gold is mystic, never blended** — is in [art-direction.md](art-direction.md)
§ Palette lock, and it is the rule this chapter breaks most.

## Every god has a canon depiction

Source of truth: `(02) 🪐 Kosmologie/Pantheons/Atakhet/` — `_index.md` for structure, one file per
god for iconography, each with a `## Darstellung` section written to be drawn. **Never invent a
god, a divine attribute or a temple rite**, and never reach for the real-world Egyptian version:
the mystic-penance plate carried an Egyptian *psychostasia* (the heart weighed against Ma'at's
feather), which is a real-world afterlife judgement, is about the dead rather than about a mystic
who fumbled, and reads as a near-miss on the name **Maatu** while getting her iconography
entirely wrong.

**The eight mystic traditions are the eight gods.** `docs/07-magic/04-mystic-spells/` lists Light,
Twilight, Life, Death, Nature, Tempest, Peace and War, mapping one-to-one onto the pantheon. A
mystic plate should know which god it is showing.

Four rules fall out of that table, each already broken once:

- **A folk's priest serves the folk's own god.** The Kerbei are *Volk von Baaphor*
  (`Kulturen/Kerbei.md`), so a Kerbei under a scales-and-judgement relief is serving the wrong
  deity — the scales are **Maatu's**, and her children are dwarves and hune. Check the culture
  file's `Pantheon:` field before casting a priest.
- **Never assemble a mixed-folk congregation at one altar.** Canon is explicit that a priest may
  only work their own god's domain and that the gods are jealous of one another, so "mystics of
  several folk kneeling in a ring" is a theological impossibility rather than a nice crowd. One
  god, that god's people. Reserve mixed casts for secular scenes.
- **Humans are the godless exception, and that is exactly why they are the arcane folk.** No
  patron, marginalised, disproportionately drawn to Nakesh in the orthodox telling. Sefkari are
  the arcane academic tradition and Khatok the arcane rebels, so a human in a *temple* scene needs
  a reason and a human at an arcane working needs none.
- **Arcane practice is a hunted crime** — see the section below.

Two things the pantheon gives away cheaply: a god's **symbol** is a better subject than the god
(Baaphor's lantern gone dark says "your power source withdrew" in one object), and **Nakesh being
officially marginal** means a pantheon frieze can carry seven carved panels and one chiselled back
to blank stone, which draws his status and costs nothing.

## Arcane practice can NEVER be staged as legitimate

`Magiesysteme/Arkane Magie.md` has it as rare, outlawed, persecuted and underground,
systematically hunted by the priestly institutions, while `Mystische Magie.md` has mystic practice
in every temple and city, socially accepted. **Only sinister cults and rogue sorcerers work arcane
magic.** Every arcane plate therefore needs three things: a **hidden or lawless place**,
practitioners who are **veiled, masked or otherwise unidentifiable**, and **someone posted as a
lookout**, facing out and away from the working.

**The trap here is a line in `docs/`, and it has cost two passes.** `01-magic-spells/index.md`
says that "since the arcane awakening of the humans, the usefulness of these arcanists as members
of the court and weapons of war was slowly acknowledged", which reads like a licence for a
legitimate court arcanist — a citadel practice yard, a patron, students. **It is not one.** The
vault is the source of truth for setting and it is unambiguous; take the docs line as flavour
about grudging usefulness, never as permission to draw an institution. If a plate has an arcanist
looking employed, it is wrong.

**Variety comes from the kind of illicit place, not from legitimacy.** Four arcane plates in one
chapter is a real problem — a run of interchangeable stone ruins — and the fix is lateral: a
buried temple hall, a subterranean city, a hidden slot canyon in badlands, a wrecked barge in reed
beds, a tar-pit camp. A canyon or a camp also buys back the register's open sky, which ruin
interiors keep spending. For a genuinely underground sanctum, see
[art-composition.md](art-composition.md) § Underground.

## A caster reads as a caster by what is in their hands

A **Jakharii war-priest of Arasha** was cast in a spell duel because Arasha is the War tradition,
and with a kilt, a bronze pectoral and a hand on a khopesh the figure read as a **warrior with a
spell attached**. The domain matched and the silhouette did not.

- **Give both hands to the working** — one on a cult implement held high, the other open and
  empty, turned toward the effect. A hand resting on a weapon reassigns the figure to the martial
  register whatever the rest of the prompt says.
- **Prefer a cult whose implement is not also a weapon.** A **light-priest of Atep** carries a
  bronze standard topped with a sun disc, and the robe-and-pectoral silhouette with both arms up
  is unmistakably a caster. Light also *purifies corruption*, so it is the honest opponent for an
  arcanist. Pick the god whose regalia reads as liturgy.

When the two systems must actually collide, neither takes any of the other's colour — the seam is
the subject, and each side gets a different *behaviour* rather than a different tint. See
[art-direction.md](art-direction.md) § Palette lock.

## Arcane corruption is a visible ladder

The cheapest way to make a sorcerer sinister — far better than a black robe, which the setting
does not have. `Arkane Magie.md` gives, in order of severity: exhaustion, **glowing veins showing
through the skin**, physical and mental mutation, then collapse. On a plate: veins lit from inside,
one hand gone hard and faceted like flaked glass with the fingers drawn too long, an eye flattened
to a pale disc — and, crucially, **the figure is untroubled by it**. Magical naturalism
([art-setting.md](art-setting.md)) applies to the practitioner as much as to the onlookers:
somebody recoiling from their own hand is a horror plate, somebody using it is this world.
