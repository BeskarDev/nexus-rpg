# Skills — step 5

Sources: `docs/03-statistics/05-skills.md`, creation step 7 in
`docs/01-basic-rules/02-character-creation.md`.

## The budget

**Seven distinct skills. Three at rank 1 (2 XP each, one talent point each). Four at rank 0.**
That is the entire starting budget, and it is the most consequential step in the build.

- Rank 1 = **Novice**: competent under normal conditions, and it is what unlocks a **talent**.
- Rank 0 = **Apprentice**: you have the skill, you roll it without the expert penalty, you get
  no talent.
- **Not recorded at all** is worse than rank 0 for the ten expert skills: an untrained expert
  skill rolls at **+1 bane**, and the two magic skills cannot be rolled untrained at all.

## The sixteen skills

**General** (no penalty when untrained): Athletics, Fortitude, Influence, Insight, Perception,
Stealth.
**Expert** (+1 bane untrained; Arcana and Mysticism impossible untrained): Arcana, Archery,
Crafting, Education, Fighting, Lore, Mysticism, Nature, Streetwise, Survival.

| Skill | Type | Rolls | Typical attribute |
|---|---|---|---|
| Athletics | General | climb, swim, jump, balance, acrobatics | STR or AGI |
| Fortitude | General | endure pain and environment, resist magic, concentrate | STR or SPI |
| Influence | General | persuade, charm, intimidate, lead | SPI |
| Insight | General | read motives, sense lies, judge people | SPI |
| Perception | General | notice, search, track by sight, initiative | SPI |
| Stealth | General | hide, move silently, sleight of hand | AGI |
| Arcana | Expert | arcane spellcasting | MND |
| Archery | Expert | ranged and thrown attacks, ranged combat arts | AGI |
| Crafting | Expert | make and repair, one crafting profession | MND or STR |
| Education | Expert | history, writing, law, formal knowledge | MND |
| Fighting | Expert | melee attacks, melee combat arts, **Parry = 7 + rank** | STR or AGI |
| Lore | Expert | myth, the supernatural, forbidden knowledge | MND |
| Mysticism | Expert | mystic spellcasting | SPI |
| Nature | Expert | animals, plants, wilderness, medicine, animal companion | MND or SPI |
| Streetwise | Expert | criminal world, cities, contacts, black markets | MND or SPI |
| Survival | Expert | travel, forage, shelter, track, endure the wild | MND or SPI |

## Picking the three rank-1 skills

**Convention: three rank-1 skills, and they are the character.** The standard shape is:

1. **The engine** — the skill the fantasy's verb is rolled with. Fighting, Archery, Arcana,
   Mysticism, or occasionally Influence/Stealth for a non-combat lead.
2. **The support beam** — the skill that keeps the engine running: Fortitude for a frontliner
   (survive), Athletics for a mobile fighter (get there), Insight for a talker (read the room),
   Lore for a caster (know what you are facing).
3. **The signature** — the skill nobody else at the table is likely to have at rank 1, and the
   one that gives the character a second job: Nature, Streetwise, Crafting, Education, Stealth.

Two more rules that decide close calls:

- **Take the talent you want, not the skill you want.** Rank 1 buys exactly one talent per
  skill, and talents are where identity lives (step 6). If a skill's rank-1 talent pool has
  nothing the build wants, that skill belongs at rank 0.
- **Never put both magic skills on one character.** A character may only ever learn ONE of
  Arcana and Mysticism.

## Picking the four rank-0 skills

Rank 0 is coverage and growth, and it is not free of consequence:

- **Fighting or Archery at rank 0 grants one combat art** (step 7) — the cheapest way to give
  a caster or a talker a real turn in a fight.
- **Fighting at rank 0 is still Parry 7**, the same as no Fighting at all — Parry is `7 +
  rank`. Take Fighting rank 0 for the art and the untrained-bane removal, not for defence.
- **An expert skill at rank 0 removes the +1 bane**, which is worth more than it sounds for a
  skill the character will roll every session (Survival on a travelling party, Streetwise in a
  city game).
- **A general skill at rank 0 buys nothing mechanically** — anyone can roll it untrained. Take
  one only when the fiction demands it, or as declared growth potential.

## Party floor test

Before locking the seven, check the character can act in all three pillars:

| Pillar | Needs at least one of |
|---|---|
| **Combat** | Fighting, Archery, Arcana, Mysticism (rank 1), or a rank-0 weapon skill with an art |
| **Exploration** | Survival, Nature, Perception, Athletics, Stealth |
| **Social** | Influence, Insight, Streetwise, Education |

Missing a pillar entirely is a design decision that must be deliberate and stated — a
character who literally cannot participate in a third of play is not a quickstart character.

## Checks before moving on

- Exactly seven distinct skills; exactly three at rank 1.
- Each rank-1 skill has a rank-1 talent the build actually wants (verify in step 6 before
  locking).
- The d8 attribute is rolled by at least one rank-1 skill.
- Not both magic skills.
- All three pillars answered, or the gap is deliberate and stated.
- Customised (`†`) skills are thematic and few — four or more means the origin pair is wrong.
